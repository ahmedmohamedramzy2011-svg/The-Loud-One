CREDITS

CREATOR & CORE INSPIRATIONS

Siren Head — Created by Trevor Henderson. Original cryptid design, siren-broadcast lore, and sound-mimicry horror that define the core antagonist of Hollow Creek.
Death Room Concept & Music — Inspired by Rooms & Doors (Roblox), origin of the dark death room, handwritten notebook + old-school CRT TV concept, and the death screen music.
Survival Tips & Entity Mechanics — Inspired by DOORS (by LSPLASH, Roblox), delivering entity-specific survival guidance and monster encounters after each defeat.

ENTITY & GAMEPLAY INSPIRATIONS

The Tenant & The Sneaky One — Hiding-spot impatience and behind-you audio/flicker ambushes inspired by Hide and Screech from DOORS (Roblox).
The Fast One (Phenomenon-242) — High-speed rusher telegraphs and locker/dumpster sprint counters inspired by A-60 (Rooms & Doors) and Rush / Ambush (DOORS).
The Moths (Phenomenon-250) — Inspired by classic Mothman cryptid folklore and light-reactive ambush horror.
The Exposure & Investigation Loop — Camera viewfinder horror and evidence-gathering stealth inspired by Fatal Frame, Outlast, Phasmophobia, Slender: The Eight Pages, and analog VHS found-footage horror.

DEVELOPMENT & TECHNOLOGY

Design, Code & Updates — The Loud One Project (Claude Code & Gemini)
3D Rendering & Audio — Three.js (WebGL) & Procedural Web Audio API
Voice Acting & Typography — Recorded player & dispatch audio (Speech Synthesis fallback); Anton, Special Elite & Caveat typefaces
